KNOW YOUR MONEY

This estimate is only one royalty type.

A recording can generate several kinds of income. Streaming revenue does not automatically include all of them.

01

Master recording royalties

Money generated by the recorded version of a song. It is normally paid to whoever owns the master recording.

02

Mechanical royalties

Money connected to reproducing or distributing a musical composition, including certain digital uses.

03

Performance royalties

Income generated when compositions are broadcast, performed publicly or used through qualifying services.

04

Neighbouring rights

Certain payments made to recording owners and performers when recorded music is publicly used or broadcast.

05

Sync royalties

Income from licensing music for films, television, advertisements, games and other visual media.

06

Producer points

A producer may receive an agreed percentage of master revenue according to the production agreement.
