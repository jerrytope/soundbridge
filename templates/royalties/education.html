Understand music income

These are general concepts. Eligibility, registrations and contracts vary by territory. Confirm your situation with the relevant organization or a qualified adviser.

Master recording
Income from use of a recording. Identify the recording owner and review distributor and label statements and agreements.
Mechanical
Composition income associated with reproduction or distribution. Record songwriter shares and check relevant registration requirements with your publisher or rights organization.
Performance
Composition income from qualifying public performance and broadcast. Confirm work registrations, writer details and reporting with the appropriate performance rights organization.
Neighboring rights
Related rights in recordings and performances. Check territorial eligibility and repertoire registration for performers and recording owners.
Synchronization
Fees for music paired with visual media. Identify both composition and master rights holders and obtain professional review of license terms.
Producer or featured-artist participation
Contractual shares depend on the agreement. Confirm the revenue basis, deductions, percentages and reporting obligations.
Direct sales and licensing
Income from downloads, physical products or direct licenses. Preserve sales and license records and identify the relevant rights and contractual shares.

Missing data or unusual amounts are possible issues to investigate, not proof of underpayment.
